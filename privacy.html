ROT DROP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

ROT DROP is a drop-and-merge game for Android, published by Koossie. This page explains what the game does with your data.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing: ROT DROP has no accounts, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and your game progress never leaves your phone.

Advertising

ROT DROP shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads: ads you choose to watch for a reward (an extra spin, double coins), and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. To serve and measure ads, these networks receive data from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. They process it under their own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Ads privacy policy

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ads. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, levels, streak, Goobdex, farm, cosmetics and settings) is saved locally on your phone. It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Reset save, or by uninstalling the app.

Updates

ROT DROP uses Google's Play In-App Updates library to ask the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version exists, and to install it. That happens between the game and the Play Store on your device; ROT DROP itself sends nothing to us. The Play Store's own handling of data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state so ads can load.
  • Advertising ID used by the ad networks above.
  • Vibration for haptic feedback on merges. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications for local reminders (free spin ready, streak ending, farm full) and a quiet notice when a new version is out. They are created on the phone itself, never sent from a server, and can be switched off in Settings or in Android's settings.
  • Run at startup so Android keeps the twice-daily update check scheduled after your phone restarts.

Children

ROT DROP is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
