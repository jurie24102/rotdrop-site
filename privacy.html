ROT DROP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

ROT DROP is an offline drop-and-merge game for Android, published by Koossie. This page explains what the game does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. ROT DROP has no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, levels, streak, Goobdex, farm, cosmetics and settings) is saved locally on your phone. It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Reset save, or by uninstalling the app.

Updates

ROT DROP uses Google's Play In-App Updates library to ask the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version exists, and to install it. That happens between the game and the Play Store on your device; ROT DROP itself sends nothing to us. The Play Store's own handling of data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Vibration for haptic feedback on merges. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications for local reminders (free spin ready, streak ending, farm full) and a quiet notice when a new version is out. They are created on the phone itself, never sent from a server, and can be switched off in Settings or in Android's settings.
  • Run at startup so Android keeps the twice-daily update check scheduled after your phone restarts.

Children

ROT DROP collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
